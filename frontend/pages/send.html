How it works

Sending to a phone number always lands in that person's default receive wallet — just like bKash.

They can shift it into any of their own wallets afterwards.

Moving funds between two of your own wallets is instant and free.

If currencies are different on either side, the amount is converted automatically using the current exchange rate.
